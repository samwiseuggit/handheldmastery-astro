## Production Standard

- Blogs: 2,000-3,000 words, one clear search intent, practical checklists or tables, five buyer questions, and at least three relevant internal links.
- Comparisons: 3,000-4,500 words, quick verdict, normalized specs, scenario winners, decision table, trade-offs, FAQ, sources, product links, and a final recommendation by buyer type.
- Images: use the exact product model and variant shown on Amazon once approved image/API access is available. Every piece needs a hero product image plus relevant in-body product images with descriptive alt text and Amazon affiliate links.
- Do not copy language, tables, or images from other comparison sites. Use independent writing and source claims from manufacturers and reputable reviews.

## Publishing Workflow

1. Confirm every product SKU, Amazon listing, and affiliate target before drafting.
2. Research current specifications, pricing, benchmarks, and availability from authoritative sources.
3. Draft to the production standard, add internal links, then attach approved product imagery.
4. Run the Astro build, verify the live page and product links, then publish.
5. Review Search Console and affiliate performance monthly; replace underperforming topics in the next month rather than repeating existing coverage.
