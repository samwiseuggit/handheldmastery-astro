## How to Choose Steam Deck Accessories Without Wasting Money

The easiest mistake is buying for a fantasy setup instead of your actual Steam Deck routine.

If you mostly play on the couch or on trips, start with protection and storage. If your Deck lives next to a monitor, keyboard, and charger, a dock matters first. If you already use your handheld gaming PC as a desktop replacement sometimes, see [How to Use a Handheld Gaming PC as a Desktop Computer](/articles/handheld-gaming-pc-desktop-setup) before you spend money on random USB-C accessories.

These four buying rules keep the list grounded:

- Buy one accessory per problem you already have.
- Prioritize the accessories that stay on the device or get used every week.
- Leave room in the budget for storage, a dock, or a case before buying comfort extras.
- Use official product pages for specs, but treat screen protectors and microSD cards as price-variable retail products when storefront stock shifts.

## FAQ

### What are the best Steam Deck accessories to buy first in 2026?

For most buyers, start with a dock, a protective case, a screen protector, and a larger microSD card. After that, add an external controller only if you regularly play docked.

### Is the official Steam Deck dock worth it over cheaper docks?

Yes if you want first-party fit, included power supply, DisplayPort 1.4, and Valve firmware support. If you mostly care about value, cheaper third-party docks still deserve a look.

### Should I buy JSAUX ModCase or dbrand Killswitch for Steam Deck?

Buy JSAUX ModCase for value and modularity. Buy dbrand Killswitch if you want the more premium, slimmer, and more expensive protective case.

### Do I need a screen protector on Steam Deck OLED?

Most owners should still use one. A tempered-glass protector is a cheap way to avoid scratches, especially if the Deck travels often or gets docked regularly.

### Is a 1TB microSD card enough for Steam Deck in 2026?

Yes for many owners. A fast 1TB microSD card is still the easiest library upgrade without opening the handheld or changing your normal portable gaming routine.

### Is the 8BitDo Ultimate 2C a good Steam Deck controller?

It is a good budget docked-play option if you also use a Windows PC or want a low-cost extra pad, but its official compatibility page focuses on Windows and Android rather than SteamOS.
